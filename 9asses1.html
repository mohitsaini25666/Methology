Section 1: Number Systems (Difficulty: Easy | Marks per question: 2)

1. Write the value of √81 and represent it on a number line.
2. Express 0.2727... in the form p/q and find its type of number.
3. Find two irrational numbers between 3 and 4.

Section 2: Polynomials (Difficulty: Medium | Marks per question: 3)

4. If (x - 1) is a factor of x² - kx + 1, find the value of k.
5. Verify whether 2 is a zero of p(x) = x³ - 3x² + 4x - 8.
6. Find the quadratic polynomial whose sum and product of zeros are -1 and -2 respectively.

Section 3: Lines and Angles (Difficulty: Hard | Marks per question: 4)

7. If two lines intersect, prove that the vertically opposite angles are equal.
8. In figure, lines AB and CD intersect at O. If ∠AOC = 70°, find ∠BOD.
9. If a transversal intersects two parallel lines, prove that alternate interior angles are equal.
