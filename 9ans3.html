Section 1: Number Systems

Q1. Which of the following is an irrational number?

Solution: √5

Q2. The decimal expansion of 1/3 is:

Solution: 0.333...

Q3. Which statement is true?

Solution: All integers are rational

Section 2: Polynomials

Q4. Which is a quadratic polynomial?

Solution: 3x² + 4x + 9

Q5. If x = 1 is a zero of p(x) = x² - 2x + 1, find p(1).

Solution: 0

Q6. The coefficient of x² in 5x² – 4x + 3 is:

Solution: 5

Section 3: Triangles

Q7. The sum of angles in a triangle is:

Solution: 180°

Q8. If triangle ABC is equilateral, then each angle equals:

Solution: 60°

Q9. Which is true about the sides of a triangle?

Solution: Sum of any two sides is greater than the third side
