Answer Sheet: Mathology Assessment 1
Class 10 Mathematics (2025 Edition)

Section 1: Real Numbers

Q1. Prove that √2 is irrational.

Solution: Proof involves contradiction — assume √2 is rational = p/q (in lowest terms), leading to both p and q even, a contradiction.

Q2. Express 0.2727... as a fraction.

Solution: 27/99 = 3/11 (Rational number)

Q3. Find two irrational numbers between 3 and 4.

Solution: √10 and √11

Section 2: Polynomials

Q4. Find the zeroes of x² - 5x + 6.

Solution: (x - 2)(x - 3) = 0 ⇒ Zeroes are 2 and 3.

Q5. Solve 3x - 4 = 11.

Solution: 3x = 15 ⇒ x = 5.

Q6. Find the quadratic polynomial with sum of zeros -1 and product of zeros -2.

Solution: Polynomial is x² + x - 2.

Section 3: Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables

Q7. Solve the system of equations: 2x + 3y = 7 and 3x - y = 5.

Solution: Solving gives x = 2, y = 1.
