Answer Sheet: Mathology Assessment 2
Class 10 Mathematics (2025 Edition)

Section 1: Real Numbers

Q1. State Euclid’s Division Lemma.

Solution: If a and b are positive integers, then there exist unique integers q and r such that a = bq + r, where 0 ≤ r < b.

Q2. Find the LCM and HCF of 6 and 20 and verify LCM × HCF = Product of numbers.

Solution: LCM = 60, HCF = 2. 6×20 = 120 and 60×2 = 120, hence verified.

Section 2: Polynomials

Q3. Find the zeroes of p(x) = x² - 4x + 3 and verify the relationship between zeroes and coefficients.

Solution: Zeroes: 1, 3. Sum = 4, Product = 3. Matches with coefficients.

Q4. Find the quadratic polynomial whose zeros are 4 and 5.

Solution: Polynomial: x² - 9x + 20

Section 3: Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables

Q5. Solve the equations 2x + 3y = 8 and 3x - 2y = -1.

Solution: x = 1, y = 2

Section 4: Quadratic Equations

Q6. Solve x² - 5x + 6 = 0 by factorization.

Solution: x = 2, x = 3

Section 5: Trigonometry

Q7. Evaluate: (sin²30° + cos²30°).

Solution: 1

Q8. If tanθ = 1, find sinθ and cosθ.

Solution: sinθ = 1/√2, cosθ = 1/√2
